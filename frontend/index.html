Welcome to DivvyUp

DivvyUp helps friends split expenses and settle debts easily.

What is DivvyUp?

DivvyUp is a simple tool for groups to keep track of who owes who. Whether you're sharing meals, trips, or rent, DivvyUp makes splitting and settling debts painless.

How it helps

  • Record expenses among friends
  • Automatically calculate balances
  • Settle up with minimal hassle
